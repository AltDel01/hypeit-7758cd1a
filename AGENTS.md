- Social posting (TikTok/Meta) uses custom per-user OAuth in edge functions (social-*), tokens encrypted in service-role-only social_connections; why: no managed connector exists and tokens must never reach the browser.
- Creative Workflow uses the shared creativeBrief helper for owner-brief language policy, four ordered narrative stages and media prompt construction; why: scanning, planning, script regeneration and media dispatch must not lose brand context or spoken lines.
- Active Creative Workflow calendar state is initialized with createWorkflowSession on mount and account changes, never rehydrated from saved strategies; why: previous visits must not repopulate the working calendar or mutate historical requests.
- Workflow product photos are private owner-scoped storage references saved on strategies and passed as actual image content or video reference media; why: every day must preserve the uploaded product rather than generate text-only substitutes.
- All image/video generation goes through supabase/functions/_shared/modelark.ts, whose GEN_PROVIDER switch (env, default alibaba) picks Alibaba DashScope or BytePlus ModelArk; auto_provider records 'alibaba' or 'modelark' so polling hits the right service. Lip sync and face swap always stay on DashScope; why: one switch flips the whole app between providers.
- Seedance rejects reference photos showing real people; wan-video rebuilds them in the background with Seedream (fast mode, cached under generated-images/<user>/ark-safe/) and resubmits; why: product photos with models must not fail with "Couldn't start".
